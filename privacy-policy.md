# NOIR Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

NOIR: Pro Video Camera is provided by iradukunda usama. This policy explains how information is handled when you use NOIR and contact support.

## Your recordings and local data

NOIR records and processes video, audio and photographs on your iPhone. Recordings, thumbnails, enhanced or graded copies, imported LUTs and camera preferences are stored locally. NOIR does not upload your footage or LUT files to the developer or RevenueCat. You choose when to share a file or save it to Photos.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Files you export to Photos or another service are handled under that service's settings and policies.

## Purchases

Apple handles billing for the optional NOIR Pro lifetime purchase. NOIR does not receive your payment card details.

NOIR uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, prevent purchase fraud and provide purchase analytics. RevenueCat processes purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, a randomly generated app user identifier, and technical information such as app/OS version, device model, locale and currency. Service providers also receive network information needed to operate their services.

NOIR does not require an account, send your name or email to RevenueCat, use advertising SDKs, or track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. Purchase information is used for app functionality and purchase analytics.

Read [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions

- Camera access enables preview, recording and still capture.
- Microphone access records sound. You can decline it and record silent video.
- Photos access is requested when you choose to save a file to Photos.
- File import accesses the video or LUT file you select.

You can manage camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

## Support and privacy requests

The optional support form asks for a name, reply email and issue description. The developer uses what you provide to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Do not include payment card information, passwords or private footage. Google hosts the form and processes submissions under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

[Contact NOIR support or make a privacy request](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScK3LiSMPpY670k-UUs4eXwGxK8gzohN3iwd_xItGT94S7I-w/viewform?usp=publish-editor).

These public documentation pages are hosted by GitHub. Visiting them is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention and your choices

Local files remain until you delete them or remove the app. Copies in Photos, other services and backups are managed separately. Purchase records may be retained to restore your entitlement, prevent fraud and meet recordkeeping requirements. Support information is retained as needed to resolve requests and related obligations.

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of information held by the developer through the support form. Some purchase records may need to be retained for access restoration or legal obligations. NOIR has no app account to delete.

## Children

NOIR is a general-purpose camera app and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact support with a privacy concern.
