# NOIR Support

Help for NOIR: Pro Video Camera, by iradukunda usama.

[Contact support](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScK3LiSMPpY670k-UUs4eXwGxK8gzohN3iwd_xItGT94S7I-w/viewform?usp=publish-editor) for technical help, purchase questions or privacy requests. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. You can use the form without attaching footage.

## Record and review

Complete or skip the introduction and allow Camera access. Tap the red record button to start and stop a take. Open Library, select a video and choose View full screen for playback. Microphone permission is optional; videos recorded without it have no audio.

## Manual controls and formats

Tap ISO, shutter or white balance to adjust them. Use the adjustment panel for focus, tint and exposure compensation. Only lenses, formats and frame rates supported by your device are offered. Tap the layout icon to switch between the clean viewfinder and full controls.

## LUT grading

Open Library, select a video, then Color Grade / LUTs. Choose a built-in look or import a compatible 3D .cube LUT, adjust Strength and compare with the source. Save graded copy creates a separate video and preserves your original. Grading supports standard SDR footage.

## Enhance a video

NOIR Pro unlocks on-device enhancement. Open a recorded take and choose Enhance Video. Processing time depends on the clip and device. Keep NOIR open during processing; going to the background cancels the job. The original remains available. HDR, Log and RAW video are not supported by the enhancement pipeline.

## Purchases and restore

NOIR Pro is an optional one-time lifetime purchase, with no subscription. The price shown in the app comes from your App Store region. Camera capture, manual controls, library playback/sharing and LUT grading remain free.

To restore, use Settings → Restore purchases or Restore on the NOIR Pro screen while signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase. If a purchase is pending approval, Pro access appears after Apple confirms it. Refund requests are managed by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

If Purchase unavailable appears, check your internet connection and try again. If it persists, contact support. A store availability error does not remove your recordings.

## Permissions and storage

Change permissions in iOS Settings → NOIR. Photos permission is requested when you save to Photos. Before removing the app, export recordings you want to keep: app removal may delete its local library. Keep enough free storage for recording and separate enhanced/graded exports.

## Privacy

Read the [NOIR Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). Footage processing happens on your iPhone. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase verification; NOIR does not receive your payment card details.
